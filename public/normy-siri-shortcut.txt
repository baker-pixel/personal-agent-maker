How to set up "Hey Siri, talk to Normy" on iPhone
====================================================

Because Normy is installed as a web app (PWA), Siri can launch it for you
with a custom Shortcut. Here's the 60-second setup:

1) Make sure Normy is on your home screen
   - Open https://normyagent.com in Safari
   - Tap the Share icon → "Add to Home Screen" → Add

2) Create the Shortcut
   - Open the Shortcuts app on your iPhone
   - Tap the "+" in the top right
   - Tap "Add Action" → search for "Open URL"
   - Paste this URL:   https://normyagent.com/decision/voice
   - Tap the Shortcut name at the top → rename to:  Talk to Normy
   - Tap "Done"

3) Assign your Siri phrase
   - Long-press the new "Talk to Normy" shortcut
   - Tap "Details" → "Add to Siri"  (or just say "Hey Siri, add ___")
   - Record your phrase (e.g. "Talk to Normy" or "Hey Normy")

4) Try it
   - Say "Hey Siri, Talk to Normy"
   - Normy will open straight into voice mode — tap the mic and start talking.

Tip: You can also pin the Shortcut to your Lock Screen or back-tap on
iPhone (Settings → Accessibility → Touch → Back Tap) for one-tap access.
